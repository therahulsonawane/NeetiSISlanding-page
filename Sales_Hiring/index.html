The Product

A Sales Intelligence System for real estate businesses.

Most real estate businesses don't have a lead problem alone. They also need to understand what's actually happening inside their sales process:

  • What happened after the lead arrived?
  • Was the follow-up actually done?
  • What did the salesperson say?
  • What objections came up?
  • Why did the prospect stop moving?
  • Which opportunities need attention?

Neeti SIS connects that entire sales journey.

Sales CRM & Pipeline

Organise leads, opportunities, follow-ups and sales activity in one system.

Sales Timeline Intelligence

See what has happened with a prospect throughout their sales journey.

AI Call Analysis

Analyse sales conversations and surface useful insights from actual calls.

Objection Intelligence

Understand recurring buyer objections and help teams respond more effectively.

Sales Practice & Coaching

Help salespeople practise conversations and improve how they handle real-world objections.

One connected system

Every module works together — from first lead to final booking.

You're not selling "another CRM." You're helping real estate businesses understand and improve how they sell.

The Person

We're not looking for just another salesperson.

We're looking for someone who wants to become an excellent closer. You may be a strong fit if:

  • You can confidently speak with business owners and decision-makers.
  • You listen and ask questions before pitching.
  • A pricing objection doesn't immediately make you offer a discount.
  • You're comfortable following up multiple times when an opportunity is genuine.
  • You can communicate clearly in English, Hindi or a natural mix of both.
  • You don't lose control when a prospect says, "I'll think about it."
  • You're comfortable learning a technology product.
  • You're comfortable being measured on actual sales outcomes.
  • You like the idea of your earnings increasing with your performance.
  • You want to build serious B2B selling skills.
We can teach you Neeti SIS. What we first want to see is how you think when a prospect says "No."
